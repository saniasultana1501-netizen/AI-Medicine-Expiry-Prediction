AI-Based Medicine Expiry Prediction and Healthcare Waste Reduction System

1. Introduction

Medicines are an important part of healthcare services. Hospitals and pharmacies maintain different types of medicines to meet patient requirements. Sometimes medicines expire before they are used due to improper stock management, overstocking and lack of timely monitoring.

This project focuses on using Artificial Intelligence, data analysis and digital tools to identify medicines that may expire soon and help reduce healthcare waste.

2. Problem Statement

Hospitals and pharmacies may face difficulties in monitoring medicine expiry dates and maintaining proper stock levels. Medicines that are not used before their expiry date can become waste and cause financial losses.

This project aims to provide a simple data-based approach to monitor medicine expiry dates and identify medicines that require attention.

3. Objectives

1. To study medicine expiry management in healthcare.
2. To identify medicines approaching their expiry dates.
3. To analyse medicine stock and expiry-related information.
4. To use AI-based analysis to support expiry risk identification.
5. To develop a Power BI dashboard.
6. To suggest ways to reduce medicine wastage.

4. Scope of the Project

The project focuses on medicine inventory monitoring and expiry management using sample or publicly available data.

It includes:

- Medicine name and category.
- Available stock quantity.
- Manufacturing date.
- Expiry date.
- Remaining shelf life.
- Expiry risk identification.
- Dashboard-based reporting.

5. Methodology

1. Collect sample medicine inventory data.
2. Organise and clean the data using Microsoft Excel.
3. Calculate the remaining shelf life of medicines.
4. Identify medicines nearing their expiry dates.
5. Use Generative AI to support data analysis.
6. Create a Power BI dashboard.
7. Provide suggestions for reducing medicine wastage.

6. Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Excel – Data entry, cleaning and calculations.
- Power BI – Dashboard creation and data visualisation.
- Generative AI – Assistance with data analysis and suggestions.
- GitHub – Project documentation and progress tracking.

7. Expected Outcomes

- Better monitoring of medicine expiry dates.
- Identification of medicines approaching expiry.
- Improved understanding of inventory management.
- Reduction in medicine wastage through timely action.
- Better reporting and decision-making.

8. Limitations

- The project uses sample or publicly available data.
- Actual hospital inventory records may not be available.
- Predictions depend on data quality.
- This is an academic project prototype.

9. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how AI-based analysis, Microsoft Excel and Power BI can support medicine expiry monitoring and healthcare waste reduction. It aims to improve inventory awareness, support timely decisions and reduce unnecessary medicine wastage.
